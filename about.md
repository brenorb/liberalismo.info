<p class="archive-kicker">Project frame</p>
<p><strong>Liberalismo.info</strong> is a collaborative project for cataloging, contextualizing, and when possible hosting full texts on liberalism and related traditions.</p>

- Project vision: `/docs/VISION/`
- Specification: `/docs/SPEC/`
- Roadmap: `/docs/ROADMAP/`

## Status
The MVP is active: structure, workflow, initial library set, and TDD gates are in place.

## How to help
- Open issues for new works or metadata corrections.
- Add verifiable primary-source references.
- Review translations and editorial consistency.
- Improve navigation, taxonomy, and search quality.
