# Liberalismo.info - Vision

## What it is
A collaborative open-source site to **catalog**, **contextualize**, and **share key texts and references** about liberalism and related traditions.

The initial focus is a **navigable archive** with clear metadata and low-friction discovery.

## Who it serves
- Readers looking for core liberal texts in original language and/or translation.
- People who want a fast reference for where a work can be found.
- Contributors who want to improve metadata and navigation quality.

## Principles
- **Archive first**: easier discovery of texts and primary sources.
- **Static-first**: long-term durability, simplicity, low operating cost.
- **Metadata rigor**: author/work/year/edition/language/source.
- **Accessibility**: lightweight pages and direct navigation.
- **Expandability**: prepared for curation layers, FAQ, and future search evolution.

## Positioning line
**"The open library of liberal thought: texts, authors, and ideas in one place."**
